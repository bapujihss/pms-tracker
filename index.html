"Fostering Academic Brilliance, Equity and Holistic Growth in Tentulikhunti"

Welcome to the official web portal of PM SHRI Government Bapuji Higher Secondary School, Tentulikhunti, Nabarangpur. Established in 1962, our institution has stood as the beacon of enlightenment in the tribal and rural heartland of Southern Odisha.

Selected as an exemplary PM SHRI institution, we are committed to national educational standards through 21st-century digital classrooms, hands-on scientific experimentation, sports infrastructure, and comprehensive government scholarship management ensuring no deserving student is left behind.

Campus Facilities & Modern Labs

PM SHRI Smart ICT Center

Equipped with 40+ networked terminals, high-speed fiber internet, interactive digital smartboards, and coding curriculum.

Composite Science Laboratories

Dedicated Physics, Chemistry and Biology experimental workstations with modern lab apparatus for both BSE & +2 CHSE practicals.

Bapuji Memorial Central Library

Over 10,000 reference books, journals, daily Odia & English newspapers, competitive exam archives, and quiet reading areas.

Green Solar Powered Campus

Hostel & Student Residence

Safe residential facilities for ST/SC and remote area scholars with hygienic mess catering and 24/7 security oversight.
